Use **“POV / camera-eye viewpoint”** rather than “first-person view,” because image models often interpret *first person* as “show the protagonist’s hands/arms.”

A stronger formulation would be:

**“Eye-level POV from the viewer’s position, as if seen directly through an invisible camera. The viewer has no visible body whatsoever. No hands, no arms, no legs, no feet, no torso, no shadow, no reflection, no weapon, no held object, no foreground body parts. The camera itself is completely invisible. Pure environmental POV only.”**

For your marketplace prompt specifically, I’d write:

> Panoramic **eye-level POV, invisible-observer camera** standing inside the marketplace. The composition should feel immersive, as though the viewer is physically present among the stalls, **but absolutely no part of the viewer appears in frame**. No hands, arms, fingers, legs, feet, clothing, weapons, baskets, held objects, shadow, reflection, or avatar silhouette. **Environment-only POV.**

The key phrases are **“invisible-observer camera”**, **“environment-only POV”**, and an explicit body-part exclusion list.

I would also avoid saying **“1st person”** entirely when you don't want avatar anatomy.
